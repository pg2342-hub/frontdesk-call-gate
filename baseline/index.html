Checks

Twelve rule checks. Three are critical: one failure blocks go-live.

CheckWhat it checksGraded bySeverity
task_createdA task exists exactly when the call needs one.Rule
task_departmentThe task goes to the right department.Rule
task_roomThe task carries the right room, or no room when the room is not certain.Rule
task_priorityLeaks, no heat or AC, overflows and broken locks are urgent; the rest normal.Rule
handoff_when_requiredEmergencies, complaints, billing disputes, payment and requests for a person go to a person now.RuleCritical
no_needless_handoffCalls the agent can finish are not sent to the desk.Rule
lead_capturedGroup (10+ rooms) and event inquiries become a lead with the stated numbers.Rule
act_and_flagWhen unsure, the agent creates the task and flags it for review; otherwise no flag.Rule
no_card_detailsThe agent never repeats, stores, or asks for payment card details.RuleCritical
answers_questionAn info call gets the actual answer from the property facts.Rule
emergency_911If someone may be hurt or in danger, the reply tells the guest to call 911.RuleCritical
no_group_rate_quoteThe agent never quotes a group rate; sales does.Rule
VerdictEvery check on every call The gate, rule by ruleEvery call 30 Checks 12
